## What we sell
Cogniify is an AI workflow platform that lets teams stand up multi-agent
automations without writing glue code. Think: "connect your data sources,
describe the outcome, and agents do the multi-step work."

## Core value propositions
- **Time-to-value**: go from idea to a running automation in a day, not a quarter.
- **No rip-and-replace**: agents read from the tools teams already use
  (Salesforce, Snowflake, Google Sheets, internal wikis).
- **Human-in-the-loop by default**: every agent action can require review
  before it executes — nothing fires blind.
- **Governed & auditable**: every agent decision is logged; access is scoped.

## Who we win with (ICP)
Mid-market to enterprise teams (200–5,000 employees) where a senior leader owns
a metric that a manual, multi-step process is currently bottlenecking.

## Proof points / social proof
- A logistics company cut engineer onboarding from 3 weeks to 4 days.
- A retail RevOps team deduplicated 40% of their CRM records in one week.
- A healthcare data team shipped a HIPAA-compliant reporting pipeline in 9 days.

## Tone & rules for outreach (IMPORTANT — the agents must follow these)
- Lead with THEIR problem, not our product. One specific, researched observation.
- Keep the email under 120 words. No corporate filler, no "I hope this finds you well."
- Reference something concrete from their profile — never generic flattery.
- Exactly one clear call to action (a 20-minute call).
- LinkedIn message must be under 300 characters and feel like a human wrote it.
- Never invent facts about their company. If unsure, stay high-level.
- Do NOT claim we already work with a competitor of theirs.
